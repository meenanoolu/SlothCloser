Why this exists

A quiet ritual for the end of the day — not another productivity system.

You dump whatever is still on your mind. SlothCloser gives you a short summary, the open loops, a soft bedtime reflection, one optional tiny thing for tomorrow, an ambient prompt — and a short poem and line to hold.

Then a clear Day closed. so you can stop carrying it.

Why open-weight / local?
Evening thoughts stay on your machine. No account. Zero ongoing cost. Swap models whenever you want. Works offline after the first pull.
